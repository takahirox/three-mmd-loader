## Known limitations (not a blanket release blocker)

Native MMD pixel-perfect matching is unverified; occasional severe PMD physics instability, PMX white clipping and Android image decoding remain under investigation. Do not attribute fixes to 0.1.4 that are not established by testing. Publishing does not imply exact native MMD parity.
